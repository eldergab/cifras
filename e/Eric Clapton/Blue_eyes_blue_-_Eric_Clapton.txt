NOTICE


The chords of this song are very easy, EC plays the E in a D shape on the 4th 
fret and all the other chords as bar chords so no open strings, he also uses 
his clasic "thumb over neck" for bass strings on bar chords. He is using a 
Martin 00028EC guitar EC signiture model which you can buy for around £6,000 
but there were only 500 made and only a handful in britain, there is one on 
sale at kingfisher music in fleet. 


One chord you will want to know is A*
