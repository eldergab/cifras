WAVE (Vou Te Contar)    - Antonio Carlos Jobim
 
=======================================================================
 
Antonio Carlos Jobim passed away yesterday in New York City.  I wanted
to pay a a tribute to him by posting this song with guitar chords.
Adeus Tom, e obrigado.
 
        --Robert St-Louis, Ottawa, 9 December 1994
